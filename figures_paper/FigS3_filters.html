Motion-energy filters of the model (Figure 5, Supplementary Figure S3)

All 80 spatiotemporal filters of the model (16 directions × 5 speed channels) in the frequency domain (fx, fy, ft): each sphere is a filter's half-height surface. The anisotropic cortex scales the cat V1 tuning widths (Li et al., 2003) by β around their mean: β = 0 is the isotropic cortex, β = 1 the cat widths, β = 2.5 the model in the paper. Drag to rotate.

Colour = preferred motion direction of the filter's unit (left red, up purple, right cyan, down green); static channels (ft = 0) paler. Black points (optional): the strongest frequencies of the rotating ring video (darker = more power, top 4 log units). Only the half-space fxcosθ + fysinθ > 0 of each filter is used, as in the model.

Response of each direction unit (its two direction-selective channels) to a grating at the filters' spatial frequency drifting at 0.58 px/frame in each direction (peak 1, times the cell number), as Figure 5A, B. The cell numbers are the cat V1 numbers scaled by α around their mean: α = 0 equal numbers, α = 1 the cat numbers (the model). With a ring video selected, black (right axis): the energy each direction unit collects from that video over the whole cycle, times the cell number, normalised to the isotropic cortex's maximum (β = 0, α = 0), as Supplementary Figure S3A-D.
